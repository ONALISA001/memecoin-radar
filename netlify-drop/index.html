📣 Narratives

What this is: an analytics page, refreshed every 60s. In Direct mode all numbers come from DexScreener's public API (prices, volume, liquidity, boosts, transactions). In Radar mode they additionally come from the Memecoin Radar engine, which computes interpretive scores and labels signals against realized outcomes. Calibration shows historical hit rates — how much trust each band has earned, nothing about future returns. Not financial advice. Memecoins are extremely high risk; most go to zero. Data may be delayed or incomplete.
