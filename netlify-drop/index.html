Real-Time Memecoin Intelligence

Not a ticker — an event-detection engine. Every token is scored every cycle across momentum, volume, order flow, whale flow, holders, liquidity and structure; when observed metrics cross thresholds, the engine emits an enriched event: what happened, how important it is, how confident we are, the supporting checks, and the contradiction to watch.

1 · MonitorLive market grid across chains — new tokens, volume acceleration, liquidity shifts, all timestamped.
2 · AnalyzeEach event carries importance (LOW→CRITICAL), a 0–100 confidence score and a why-checklist of the indicators that fired.
3 · DetectOrdered signal chains surface compound setups — e.g. volume → buy pressure → holders → momentum — before they trend.
