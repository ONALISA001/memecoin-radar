What this is: an analytics page sampling the Memecoin Radar API every 60s. Scores († Trend / FOMO / Risk / Organic 0–100) are interpretive composites of public market data (DexScreener) and social telemetry. “Organic” measures wash-trading / sybil / coordination heuristics, not legitimacy of the asset. Calibration shows historical hit rates — they say how much trust each band has earned, nothing about future returns. Not financial advice. Memecoins are extremely high risk; most go to zero. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or (in this demo's early history) partly simulated.
